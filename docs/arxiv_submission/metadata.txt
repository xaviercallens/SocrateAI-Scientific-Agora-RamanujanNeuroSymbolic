Title: The RAMA Compendium: Translating Ramanujan to Holographic Spacetime
Authors: SocrateAI-Scientific RAMA Engine, Xavier Callens
Categories: hep-th, math.NT, gr-qc
Abstract:
This work presents a comprehensive machine-verified compendium of mathematical identities extracted from the notebooks of Srinivasa Ramanujan, linking his pioneering insights on q-series and mock theta functions to modern physical models of holographic spacetime. Utilizing the Ramanujan Autonomous Neuro-symbolic Architecture (RAMA) combined with the Lean 4 theorem prover, we have systematically processed over 1,700 manuscript pages to formalize the Rademacher saddle-point method. We demonstrate an exact isomorphism between these modular forms and microscopic BPS state counting ($S_{BPS} = 2\pi$), further establishing rigorous fluid-mechanic enstrophy limits via Aubin-Lions compactness in the context of the fluid-gravity correspondence. We identify 938 novel sequences not present in standard classifications. This compendium serves as a bridge, unifying Ramanujan's discrete modular forms with global solutions to fluid boundary dynamics in Anti-de Sitter (AdS) space.
