Title: The Ramanujan Neuro-Symbolic Engine (v3.2): Exact q-Series Invariants, All-n Lean 4.33.1 Kernel Certificates, and R1-R5 Epistemic Audit
Authors: Xavier Callens
Categories: math.NT, hep-th
Comments: 14 pages, 2 figures. Includes post-audit R1-R5 remediation, Campaign v2 complete 839-page Ramanujan corpus report (campaign_v2_report.json), T2 validation report (llm_lean_validation_report.json), and 338 live zero-custom-axiom Lean 4.33.1 kernel theorems across CampaignV2Verified.lean (275) and AllNAndPropositionsVerified.lean (63).
Abstract:
We present a neuro-symbolic research program and formal verification audit coupling multimodal extraction from Srinivasa Ramanujan's manuscripts, exact integer/rational q-series arithmetic, literature-grounded RAG synthesis (24 arXiv papers), and Lean 4 formalization under strict epistemic rules (R1-R5: zero custom axioms, no untracked sorry placeholders, anti-vacuity enforcement, verified citations, and exact rational certificates). First, we document the historical three-finding verification audit of the legacy RAMA-LS formalization tree and its complete post-audit remediation (reducing live custom axioms from 34 to 0, sorry placeholders from 2 to 0, vacuous proofs from 13 to 0, reconciling M2 arithmetic certificates, and installing GitHub Actions CI enforcement). Second, we report Campaign v2 and Wave 4 across all 839 Ramanujan manuscript pages in the repository (133 Narosa 1988 Lost Notebook facsimile sheets, 8 letters from Ono, and 698 pages of Notebooks 1-3; 0 extraction errors). Out of 2,458 extracted identities, deterministic Mobius inversion and symbolic q-series evaluation isolate 54 unique Dedekind eta-quotients (25 satisfying Ono Theorem 1.64 congruences on Gamma_0(N) and 37 with non-negative Fourier coefficients), 5 Ramanujan LHS=RHS identities, 10 exact classical Tier B matches (mock theta functions of orders 3, 5, 6 and Rogers-Ramanujan G(q)), and 14 OCR-corrupted equations automatically refuted in exact Q[[q]] arithmetic. Two standalone Init-only Lean 4.33.1 modules yield 338 live zero-custom-axiom kernel theorems with #print axioms footprints strictly inside {propext}: CampaignV2Verified.lean (275 theorems) and AllNAndPropositionsVerified.lean (63 theorems, establishing universal all-n and all-len proofs for four Ramanujan identities, conditional Jacobi Triple Product lifts, level-12 weight-1 Nebentypus (-3/d) and Sturm-bound conditional verification at B(Gamma_0(12),1)=2<15, universal EtaQuot algebra, live Deep Burn kernel invariants, universal sym2_recurrence_int, exact Q+ T-duality, and certified Tier X refutations of M2, M3, S12 mock-proxy claims, and 14 OCR typos). Explicit epistemic boundaries separate unconditional Mathlib analytic proofs and offline lake builds from live kernel certificates and maintain Tier C conditional status for Hypothesis U under the BHMR slow-derivative regime and Tao's averaged Navier-Stokes blowup barrier.
