\section{The Proposal}\label{sec:proposal}
For most of its history, rigorous mathematics has proceeded bottom-up:
deriving $C$ from $A$ and $B$. Ramanujan's working style --- documented most
starkly in the \emph{Lost Notebook} \cite{Narosa1988, AndrewsBerndt} --- was different: identities were recorded as endpoints, with the connective proofs
supplied by later generations. We take that style as \emph{design
inspiration}, not as evidence for any particular cognitive model, and propose a three-stage pipeline:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The heuristic engine (\textsc{Rama}).} Mathematical candidate
generation is treated as local search over a symbolic space of Eulerian
$q$-series, guided by an energy functional
$E=\alpha C+\beta I+\gamma D$.
\item \textbf{The shadow bridge.} Candidate mock-symmetries are completed to
harmonic Maass forms by computing their non-holomorphic period integrals \cite{Zwegers2002}.
\item \textbf{The formal lock.} Surviving statements are formalized in
Lean~4 under the audit rules of Section 2.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Conjectural Dictionary}\label{sec:dictionary}
The program's core object is a proposed dictionary between the $\sqrt{\alpha'}$-regularized dynamics of an incompressible flow on a macroscopic manifold and arithmetic structures carried by a quantum fiber.

In the heuristic motivation of the program, this mapping draws on the \textbf{Fluid/Gravity correspondence} \cite{BHMR2008, Rangamani2009}, which derives relativistic conformal hydrodynamics as a long-wavelength, slow-derivative expansion of black-brane geometries in $\mathrm{AdS}_5$. Crucially, however, this correspondence is valid only in a \emph{small-gradient hydrodynamic regime} that breaks down precisely when velocity gradients and enstrophy diverge. Moreover, Tao's construction of finite-time blowup for an averaged three-dimensional Navier--Stokes equation preserving the exact energy identity and dyadic shell estimates \cite{Tao2016} demonstrates that no generic energy or Aubin--Lions compactness argument can establish global 3D regularity without exploiting fine geometric cancellation in the true nonlinearity. Consequently, any holographic link between fluid enstrophy bounds and Ramanujan $\eta$-quotients remains strictly conditional and open (\tierC{}). Similarly, while Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak \cite{LPS1988} and Marcus--Spielman--Srivastava \cite{MSS2015} establish genuine $k$-regular Ramanujan graphs via Deligne's bound \cite{Deligne1974} and interlacing polynomials, our tested M2 triad circulant graphs violate the Alon--Boppana bound and are classified as \tierX{} (Falsified).

\subsection{Hydrodynamic limits and Ramanujan's sums of tails}

There exists $C>0$, independent of $\alpha'$, such that the enstrophy
density of the $\sqrt{\alpha'}$-truncated flow satisfies
$\sup_{t}\;\alpha'\!\cdot\!\mathcal{E}_{\alpha'}(t)\le C$ \cite{BKM1984, CKN1982, CF1993, CFM1996}, and the truncation defect of the energy flux
admits an exact sums-of-tails representation \cite{AJO2001, BowmanMcLaughlin2004}. In Lean~4, \texttt{HypothesisU.lean} and \texttt{MasterDualScale.lean} formalize only conditional implications taking Hypothesis~U (\tierC{}) and Aubin--Lions compactness (\tierL{}) as explicit hypothesis parameters, consistent with the hydrodynamic-limit boundary \cite{BHMR2008, Rangamani2009} and Tao's barrier \cite{Tao2016}.

\subsection{$K3$ geometry, Mathieu moonshine, and the $\operatorname{Sym}^{2}(L_{2})$ lock}
\paragraph{Algebraic Lock (\tierA{}) and $S_{12}$ Provenance (\tierX{}, Quarantined Mock-Proxy).}

\section{Mixed-Cyclotomic Forms and BPS State Counting on $K3 \times T^2$}

\subsection{Thermodynamic Saddle Point Matrix and Corrected Sign Convention}
We rely on the Rademacher circle method to evaluate the asymptotic growth of integer partition generating functions. For a Dedekind $\eta$-quotient
\begin{equation}
f(\tau) = \prod_{d \mid N} \eta(d\tau)^{r_d}
\end{equation}
of level $N = \operatorname{lcm}\{d : r_d \neq 0\}$ and modular weight $k = \frac{1}{2}\sum_d r_d$, the cusp-$0$ and general cusp-$c$ ($c \mid N$) effective central charges are given by:
\begin{equation}
c_{\text{eff}}(0) = -\sum_{d} \frac{r_d}{d}, \qquad
c_{\text{eff}}(c) = -\frac{1}{c^2} \sum_{d} \frac{\gcd(d, c)^2}{d} r_d, \qquad
E_0 = -\frac{1}{24} \sum_{d} d\, r_d.
\end{equation}
With this physically consistent sign convention, the bosonic integer partition generating function $1/\eta(\tau)$ ($r_1 = -1$) carries positive central charge $c_{\text{eff}}(0) = +1$, whereas Euler's pentagonal product $\eta(\tau)$ ($r_1 = +1$) and Ramanujan's cusp form $\Delta(\tau) = \eta(\tau)^{24}$ ($r_1 = 24$) carry negative cusp-$0$ values ($c_{\text{eff}}(0) = -1$ and $-24$, respectively) and alternating-sign Fourier coefficients.

\subsection{Legacy Phase 2 Genetic Attractors (\tierC{} Erratum)}

Earlier Phase~2 drafts of this section reported three heuristic Notebook~2 attractors evaluated under an inverted sign formula ($+\sum r_d/d$) \cite{EOT2011}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $f_1(\tau) = \eta(6\tau)^6\,\eta(9\tau)^{-2}\,\eta(11\tau)^{-4}$ ($k = 0$): under the corrected sign convention, $c_{\text{eff}}(0) = -\frac{41}{99} \approx -0.414 < 0$ (rather than $+0.414$).
    \item $f_2(\tau) = \eta(6\tau)^3\,\eta(12\tau)^2$ ($k = 5/2$): because all exponents are positive, $c_{\text{eff}}(0) = -\frac{2}{3} \approx -0.667 < 0$ and its Fourier series is an alternating-sign index rather than a positive-definite state count \cite{Zwegers2002}.
    \item $f_3(\tau) = \eta(\tau)^1\,\eta(5\tau)^2\,\eta(10\tau)^3$ ($k = 3$): similarly, $c_{\text{eff}}(0) = -\frac{17}{10} = -1.700 < 0$ (oscillating cusp-like coefficients rather than a positive-definite thermal CFT spectrum).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Connection to the cross-stream thesis}

The pattern across Streams~0--6 is as follows.
A stream declares a verification status (\textit{``Zero-Sorry''},
\textit{``machine-checked''}, \textit{``CI-gated''}), and that
declaration is not produced by the mechanism the reader would expect.
In RAMA-LS, three independent mechanisms contribute to the gap:
\begin{enumerate}

    is easily missed, and whose isolation from the build is exactly what

  \item A live tree with 2 \texttt{sorry}, 34 \texttt{axiom}, and at
    least one pinned constant\,---\,none of which prevents compilation,
    but each of which qualifies any Tier~A claim that depends on it
    (Finding~2).
  \item A README that names a CI file as the enforcement mechanism,
    while that file is not connected to a runner (Finding~3).
\end{enumerate}

RAMA-LS is the best-evidenced instance of this pattern in our corpus
precisely because all three sub-findings were confirmed in a single direct

live-tree \texttt{axiom} count at a figure twenty times larger than the

The commit message and the commit contents are directly contradictory.
No adjective is needed: the fact speaks for the pattern.

\subsection*{Why this was not caused by a bad decision}

None of the findings above reflects a methodological error.
They reflect \emph{ordering}: the claims came first and the checking came
later.
The RAMA engine generates $\eta$-quotient candidates, the scaffolds
formalize them, and verification was always the final step.
That ordering is neither unusual nor wrong\,---\,it is how mathematics
has always been done.
The error is only that the status strings (``Zero-Sorry,'' ``CI-enforced'')
arrived at the same time as the scaffolds and stayed after the checking
stopped short.
This is already the argument in \texttt{NEW\_STREAM.md}, and putting it
here is what makes this section evidence for a general structural pattern
rather than an accusation directed at one tree.
