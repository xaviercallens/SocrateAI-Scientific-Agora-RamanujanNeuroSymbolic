\section{Introduction}

The convergence of algebraic geometry and quantum information has opened new avenues for understanding macroscopic phenomena. Specifically, the relationship between Ramanujan's mock modular forms, the BPS state counts of K3 surface compactifications, and the emergence of macroscopic Fuzzy Dark Matter (FDM) forms the core of our Dual-Scale framework. In this paper, we consolidate the mathematical foundations of this relationship, verified computationally via Lean 4, and demonstrate its native mapping into holographic tensor networks.

By bypassing classical differential geometric approaches and utilizing exact rational arithmetic, we establish a rigid bridge from microscopic string states (UV) to observable macroscopic physics (IR).
