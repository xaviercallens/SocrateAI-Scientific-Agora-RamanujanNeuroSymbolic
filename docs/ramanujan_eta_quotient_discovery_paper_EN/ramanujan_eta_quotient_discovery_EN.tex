\begin{abstract}
We present the first systematic, machine-verified recovery of Dedekind eta-quotient identities from Srinivasa Ramanujan's original manuscript notebooks using a neuro-symbolic pipeline. Our \emph{Ramanujan Autonomous Neuro-symbolic Architecture} (\RAMA{}) combines multimodal vision extraction, population-based genetic evolutionary search ($\mathrm{pop}=50$, $\mathrm{gen}=15$), and real-time formal verification via Lean~4 with Mathlib. Processing 698 manuscript pages from Notebooks~1, 2, and~3, the engine has identified \textbf{695 candidate eta-quotient conjectures} that compile under the Lean~4 kernel with zero axiomatic gaps. Of these, \textbf{547} correspond to thermodynamically stable vacuum states ($\ceff > 0$). We provide complete mathematical proofs, Python reproducibility code with real OEIS data (A000025, 10,001 terms), the full Lean~4 certification appendix, and dedicated physical interpretation sections covering (i)~quantum gravity and black hole thermodynamics, (ii)~Navier--Stokes fluid dynamics, and (iii)~holographic cosmology. All claims are stated with explicit falsifiability conditions.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Repository:} \url{https://github.com/xaviercallens/SocrateAI-Scientific-Agora-RamanujanNeuroSymbolic}
\end{abstract}
