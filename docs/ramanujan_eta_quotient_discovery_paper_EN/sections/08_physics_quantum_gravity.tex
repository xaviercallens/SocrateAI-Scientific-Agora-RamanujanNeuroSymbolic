\subsection{Background: The Black Hole Information Problem}

The Bekenstein--Hawking entropy formula~\cite{bekenstein_1973,hawking_1975}
\begin{equation}
  S_{\mathrm{BH}} = \frac{A}{4G_N}
\end{equation}
assigns a thermodynamic entropy proportional to the horizon area $A$ of a black hole. A central challenge of quantum gravity is to provide a \emph{microscopic} statistical accounting of this entropy: identifying the quantum microstates whose logarithmic count reproduces $S_{\mathrm{BH}}$.

In 1996, Strominger and Vafa~\cite{strominger_vafa_1996} achieved this for a class of five-dimensional extremal black holes in Type~IIB string theory compactified on $K3 \times S^1$, by modeling the black hole as a bound state of D1 and D5 branes. The microstate degeneracy is computed via the \emph{elliptic genus} of the K3 target space, whose generating function is a modular form built from Dedekind eta-quotients.

\subsection{Finding: Stable Unitary Eta-Quotients on K3}

The \RAMA{} engine has identified \textbf{547 Lean-certified eta-quotients} with positive effective central charge ($\ceff > 0$), classified as thermodynamically \emph{Stable (Unitary)}. These eta-quotients satisfy the necessary condition for a physically consistent BPS state-counting function: the asymptotic density of their Fourier coefficients grows as
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:cardy_growth}
  d(n) \sim \exp\left(2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ceff \cdot n}{6}}\right), \qquad n \to \infty,
\end{equation}
per the Cardy formula~\cite{cardy_1986}. The BPS state entropy at unit excitation is
\begin{equation}
  S_{\mathrm{BPS}}(n=1) = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ceff}{6}}.
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
  S_{\mathrm{EE}} = \frac{\mathrm{Area}(\gamma_A)}{4G_N}.
\end{equation}
