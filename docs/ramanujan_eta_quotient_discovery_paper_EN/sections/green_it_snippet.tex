\subsection{Computational Footprint}
\label{subsec:green_it}

The full \RAMA{} nightly run was executed on a single laptop-class CPU (estimated TDP: 65W).

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Value} \\
\midrule
Total runtime & 1.13~hours \\
Pages processed & 698 \\
Lean-verified discoveries & 694 \\
Pages per hour & 618.3 \\
Total energy & 0.0734~kWh \\
Energy per discovery & 0.11~Wh \\
CO$_2$ (France grid) & 4.1~g \\
CO$_2$ (US grid) & 28.6~g \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The entire 698-page corpus was processed with a carbon footprint of 0.004~kg CO$_2$ (French nuclear grid, 56~g/kWh), equivalent to approximately 0.0~km driven in a passenger car. This demonstrates that neuro-symbolic mathematical discovery is computationally accessible on consumer hardware.
