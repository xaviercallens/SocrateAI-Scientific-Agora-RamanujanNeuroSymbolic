\begin{abstract}
\noindent We outline a research program that seeks \emph{exact algebraic
surrogates} --- drawn from $q$-series, mock modular forms, and the arithmetic
of $K3$ surfaces --- for the analytic estimates that govern regularized fluid
dynamics and related dual-scale geometries. The program couples (i) a
heuristic search and exact $\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Q}$ inversion engine (\textsc{Rama}) over Eulerian $q$-series, (ii) shadow
completion in the sense of Zwegers, and (iii) formalization in the Lean~4
proof assistant under a strict audit discipline (Rules R1--R5). This version (3.2) documents the historical three-finding verification audit of the legacy codebase, its complete post-audit remediation eliminating all custom axioms, untracked \texttt{sorry} placeholders, and vacuous proofs, and the Wave~4 kernel upgrades totaling \textbf{338} live zero-custom-axiom Lean~4.33.1 theorems across \texttt{CampaignV2Verified.lean} (275 theorems over 54 unique $\eta$-quotients and 5 identities) and \texttt{AllNAndPropositionsVerified.lean} (63 theorems establishing universal $\forall n$ / $\forall\text{len}$ proofs, conditional Jacobi Triple Product lifts, level-12 weight-1 Nebentypus and Sturm-bound verification at $B(\Gamma_0(12),1)=2<15$, live RAMA Deep Burn invariants, universal \texttt{EtaQuot} algebra, $\operatorname{Sym}^2(L_2)$, exact $\mathbb{Q}^+$ T-duality, and certified refutations of M2, M3, $S_{12}$ proxy claims, and 14 OCR typos).
