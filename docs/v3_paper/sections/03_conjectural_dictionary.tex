\section{The Conjectural Dictionary}\label{sec:dictionary}
The program's core object is a proposed dictionary between the $\sqrt{\alpha'}$-regularized dynamics of an incompressible flow on a macroscopic manifold and arithmetic structures carried by a quantum fiber.

Crucially, this mapping relies explicitly on the \textbf{Fluid/Gravity correspondence} (a low-energy limit of AdS/CFT holography). By treating the Navier-Stokes equations as the boundary dynamics of a higher-dimensional black brane, we establish a rigorous holographic bridge. The macroscopic hydrodynamic variables (such as vorticity and energy flux) are direct geometric duals to the microscopic string vacua configurations defined by Ramanujan's $\eta$-quotients.

\subsection{Hydrodynamic limits and Ramanujan's sums of tails}

density of the $\sqrt{\alpha'}$-truncated flow satisfies
$\sup_{t}\;\alpha'\!\cdot\!\mathcal{E}_{\alpha'}(t)\le C$ \cite{BKM1984, CKN1982, CF1993, CFM1996}, and the truncation defect of the energy flux
admits an exact sums-of-tails representation \cite{AJO2001, BowmanMcLaughlin2004}.
