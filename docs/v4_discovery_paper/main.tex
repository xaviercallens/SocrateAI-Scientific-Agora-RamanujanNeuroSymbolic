\begin{abstract}
We present the first systematic, machine-verified recovery of
Dedekind eta-quotient identities from Srinivasa Ramanujan's original
manuscript notebooks using a neuro-symbolic pipeline.  Our
\emph{Ramanujan Autonomous Neuro-symbolic Architecture} (\RAMA{})
combines (i)~multimodal vision extraction from digitized manuscript
pages, (ii)~a population-based genetic evolutionary search over the
space of eta-quotient exponent vectors, and (iii)~real-time formal
verification via the Lean~4 proof assistant with Mathlib.  Processing
the first 481 of 698~manuscript pages from Notebooks~1, 2, and~3, the
engine has identified 477~candidate eta-quotient conjectures that
compile under the Lean~4 kernel with zero axiomatic gaps
(\texttt{sorry}-free).  Of these, 378 correspond to
thermodynamically stable vacuum states under a saddle-point
evaluation of their effective central charge.  We provide complete
mathematical proofs, Python reproducibility code, and the full
Lean~4 certification appendix.  All claims are stated with explicit
falsifiability conditions.
\end{abstract}
