\section{Physical Interpretation and Conjectured Applications}
\label{sec:physics}

\subsection{Preamble: Scope and Epistemic Status}

The physical interpretations presented in this section are
\emph{conjectural mappings} motivated by the structural
parallels between eta-quotient invariants and conformal
field theory quantities.  They do not constitute proven
physical predictions and are subject to the falsifiability
conditions stated in \cref{sec:limitations}.

\subsection{BPS State Counting on K3 Surfaces}

In Type~IIB string theory compactified on
$K3 \times S^1$~\cite{strominger_vafa_1996}, the
degeneracy of $1/2$-BPS states at charge level $n$ is
governed by a generating function of the form
\begin{equation}
  Z(\tau) = \sum_{n \geq 0} d(n) \, q^n
  = \prod_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(1-q^n)^{24}}
  = \frac{1}{\eta(\tau)^{24}} \cdot q,
\end{equation}
where $d(n)$ counts the number of BPS microstates and
the exponent $24$ reflects the Euler characteristic of
K3~\cite{dijkgraaf_verlinde_verlinde}.

Our discovered eta-quotients---particularly the 377~stable
unitary candidates---can be viewed as \emph{generalized}
generating functions of the form
\begin{equation}
  Z_{\mathbf{r}}(\tau) = q^{p}
  \prod_{d=1}^{d_{\max}} \eta(d\tau)^{r_d},
\end{equation}
whose Fourier coefficients may count states in
\emph{orbifold} compactifications or heterotic duals
of K3~\cite{dabholkar_2005}.

\subsection{Tachyonic States and Swampland Constraints}

The 101~candidates with $\ceff < 0$ are classified as
``Tachyonic.''  In the string theory landscape,
tachyonic states signal an unstable vacuum that either
decays or is in the \emph{swampland}---the set of
effective field theories that cannot be UV-completed
into a consistent theory of quantum
gravity~\cite{strominger_vafa_1996}.

\begin{conjecture}[Swampland Filter]
\label{conj:swampland}
Eta-quotients with $\ceff < 0$ that appear in Ramanujan's
manuscripts encode partition functions of string vacua in
the swampland.  They are ``rejected'' candidates that
Ramanujan may have considered and discarded.
\end{conjecture}

\textbf{Falsifiability:} This conjecture is falsified if
a tachyonic candidate is shown to arise as the partition
function of a known, consistent string compactification.

\subsection{Connection to Mathieu Moonshine}

The sporadic group $M_{24}$ acts on the K3 elliptic
genus, and the multiplicities of its representations
are encoded in eta-quotients and mock modular
forms~\cite{ono2004}.  Several of our discovered
candidates have weight $k = 3/2$ or $k = 8$, which are
precisely the weights that appear in the context of
Mathieu moonshine.

\begin{remark}
We do not claim that our candidates exhibit moonshine
phenomena.  A rigorous check would require computing
the McKay--Thompson series for each relevant conjugacy
class of $M_{24}$ and comparing against our $q$-expansions.
This is deferred to future work.
\end{remark}
